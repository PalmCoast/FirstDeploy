How to choose for a small shop

  • Lowest monthly price, and you'll set it up yourself: look at Rosie or Goodcall.
  • You want a live human available on hard calls: look at Smith.ai.
  • You run ServiceTitan and want AI booking onto the board: look at Sameday or Avoca.
  • You want outbound sales calling: look at RepForce.ai.
  • You want it built, tested and tuned for you at one flat price: that's what First Deploy does.

Questions to ask any vendor, including us

  • What does it say on a gas-smell or water-through-the-ceiling call? Ask to hear it.
  • Does it read the callback number back?
  • Will it quote prices or promise arrival times? Can you turn that off?
  • What happens to the bill on your busiest week?
  • Who changes the script when you need it changed, and how fast?

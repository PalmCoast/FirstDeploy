Lots of AI phone products say they're easy to set up. "Done for you" should mean you don't do the setup at all. Here's exactly what's included when First Deploy builds your after-hours desk, step by step, so you can compare it with anything else you're considering.

1. A written plan

Before any work starts, we write down your greeting, the questions the desk asks, your emergency rules and safety steps, what it must never say, where call details go, and the go-live date. $875 starts the work.

2. A build in your shop's name

  • Answers with your business name, hours, service area, and the work you do and don't take.
  • Asks for name, callback number (read back to confirm), service address, the problem and how soon.
  • Keeps your existing number. You forward it after hours or when you don't pick up, and we walk you through it.
  • Recording notice at the start of every call. It says it's an automated assistant if asked.
  • No quotes, arrival promises, discounts or your cell number unless you say so.

3. Testing before go-live

We put it through practice calls against hard callers (angry, confused, emergencies, robocalls) and fix what breaks before your customers ever hear it.

4. Go live and keep tuning

You forward your line. The balance is due at completion. From go-live it's $250/mo, which covers ongoing tuning and changes, and you call the person who built it instead of a ticket queue.

What's not included

We don't promise more jobs or a revenue number. The desk doesn't staff live humans. Calendar booking isn't standard; if you want calls booked straight into a calendar, we scope that in your written plan.

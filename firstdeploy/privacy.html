Privacy

First Deploy is a product of AgentHive Inc in Palm Coast, Florida. This page covers firstdeploy.ai. We do not publish a street address.

What we collect

This site does not host its own lead form. If you call, we get the number you dial from. If you book the free 30, Calendly collects the name, email, and time you submit there. If you pay, Stripe collects what it needs to run the charge. Leak-check pages (missed calls, dead quotes, no-shows) are separate sites with their own forms.

How it is used

We use that information to answer you, book time, invoice, and do the work you hired. We do not sell it. We do not run a marketing list from this page.

Hosting and analytics

The site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may record operational request logs and, when enabled, Real User Monitoring (page performance). That is for keeping the site up, not for building an ad profile.

Third parties you may hit from this site

Links and embeds can send you to Calendly, Stripe, and Google Fonts. Those services have their own policies. This deploy does not inject third-party shop strips or ad units.

Cookies

This site’s own pages do not set a first-party advertising cookie. We do not show a cookie banner for that reason. Third-party embeds (Calendly, payment hosts) may set their own cookies when you use them.

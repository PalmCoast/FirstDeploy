Why billing model matters after hours

With per-call billing, a stormy night or a week of no-heat calls means a bigger bill. Smith.ai explains on its own page that it bills per call rather than per minute so call length doesn't drive cost. With First Deploy the monthly is the same $250 whether the desk takes five calls or fifty.

When Smith.ai is the better pick

If you want a free tier to start, a live person available on hard calls, or fully human answering, Smith.ai offers those and we don't. If your after-hours volume is very low, its Free tier (25 calls a month) may cost less than our monthly.

When First Deploy is the better pick

If you want one predictable number, you don't want to build or prompt the AI yourself, and you want the desk written around trade-shop emergencies by the person you'll call for changes.

First Deploy, in one paragraph

First Deploy builds and runs an after-hours AI phone desk for trade shops. We write the script with you, set it up on the number you already have (you forward it after hours or when you don't pick up), test it with practice calls before go-live, and keep tuning it while the monthly is on. It answers in your shop's name, takes the caller's name, callback number (read back), address, problem and urgency, flags emergencies under rules you approve, and sends you the details right away. By default it never quotes prices, promises arrival times or gives out your cell, and every call opens with a recording notice. Price: $1,750 setup ($875 to start, balance at completion), then $250/mo from go-live. Month to month. No per-minute or per-call charge.

Questions owners ask

How does Smith.ai charge?

Per call. Its AI Receptionist has a free tier of 25 calls a month and paid plans from $150 a month; its live receptionist plans start at $300 a month for 30 calls (checked October 19, 2026).

Does First Deploy charge per call?

No. It's $1,750 setup ($875 to start, balance at completion), then $250 a month from go-live, with no per-call or per-minute charge.

Does First Deploy transfer calls to live people?

No. First Deploy is an AI desk. It takes the details, flags emergencies under the rules you approve, and sends everything to you right away. If you need live humans on calls, Smith.ai's live plans are built for that.

Can I cancel?

First Deploy's monthly is month to month and can be cancelled any time. Smith.ai's pages say both of its services are month to month.

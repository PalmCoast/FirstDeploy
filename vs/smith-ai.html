How Smith.ai answers

On the AI Receptionist page, every tier lists 24/7 call handling. Free includes recording, transcription, summaries, lead qualification, routing, scheduling (Calendly and others), and integrations including Clio and Zapier. Calls start with the AI. A complex call can be transferred to a live North America-based receptionist. Calls from “20 million+ known spammers” are filtered before the AI answers and do not count toward the quota.

The live receptionist page is a separate product: “Professional call answering by real people,” live-staffed 24/7, with “No overseas agents.” Appointment booking on that page is an add-on at $1.50 per call.

Setup

AI Free is self-serve, and custom AI prompting is marked “Not included.” AI Pro says “Set it up yourself with dedicated support,” plus dedicated onboarding and live phone, email, and chat support. AI Enterprise includes full-service setup, custom prompting, custom integrations, multi-location support, and a dedicated success manager. The live plans say “No setup fees.” First Deploy’s setup is a separate $1,750, for one leak, live this week on a written plan.

When Smith.ai is the better choice

Smith.ai’s AI Receptionist is the better choice if you want the free 25-call tier before you pay, and you want the bill to follow call count. The Pro and Enterprise tables step the per-call rate down as the allowance grows. It is also the better choice if a hard call should transfer to Smith.ai’s live receptionist network. Their AI page describes that handoff. First Deploy takes the details, books, and follows up. It does not staff that live network.

The live receptionist plans are the better choice if you want a person on every call. They start at $300 for 30 calls, with no setup fee, and they list Clio, HubSpot, and Salesforce among CRM handoffs, plus a dedicated number or a port at no charge. A firm that wants that intake stack is the buyer those pages describe.

First Deploy is the fit when the job is one after-hours leak for a trade shop — HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, pest, landscaping, or auto and truck repair — installed for $1,750 and then left at $250 a month, with no per-call line.

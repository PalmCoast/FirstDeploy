When Goodcall is the better pick

If you're happy building and editing your own agent, Goodcall's published monthly prices are lower than ours and there's no setup fee. If most of your callers are repeat customers, the unique-customer count stays small.

When First Deploy is the better pick

If you'd rather not build it yourself and want one number that doesn't change on a busy storm night, First Deploy's setup covers the script, emergency rules and practice-call testing, and the $250 monthly covers ongoing tuning.

First Deploy, in one paragraph

Questions owners ask

Does Goodcall charge per minute?

No. Goodcall's pricing page says it never meters minutes. It bills per unique customer above each plan's monthly allowance (checked October 19, 2026).

What does Goodcall cost?

Starter is $79 a month for 100 unique customers, then 79¢ each. Growth is $129 for 500, then 26¢. Scale is $299 for 2,000, then 15¢. Check goodcall.com/pricing for current numbers.

What does First Deploy cost?

$1,750 setup ($875 to start, balance at completion), then $250 a month from go-live. No per-caller or per-minute charge. Month to month.

Who sets up First Deploy?

We do. We write the script and emergency rules with you, set it up on your existing number, and test it with practice calls before go-live.

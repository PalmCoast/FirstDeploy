When RepForce is the better pick

If you want AI reps doing outbound calling, lead follow-up and sales conversations at volume, with CRM integrations and dashboards, RepForce is built for that and we aren't.

When First Deploy is the better pick

If the problem is calls at 9 p.m. going to voicemail, you probably don't need a sales platform. You need a desk that answers in your shop's name, handles emergencies by your rules and sends you a clean job record, for a flat price that's on the page.

First Deploy, in one paragraph

First Deploy builds and runs an after-hours AI phone desk for trade shops. We write the script with you, set it up on the number you already have (you forward it after hours or when you don't pick up), test it with practice calls before go-live, and keep tuning it while the monthly is on. It answers in your shop's name, takes the caller's name, callback number (read back), address, problem and urgency, flags emergencies under rules you approve, and sends you the details right away. By default it never quotes prices, promises arrival times or gives out your cell, and every call opens with a recording notice. Price: $1,750 setup ($875 to start, balance at completion), then $250/mo from go-live. Month to month. No per-minute or per-call charge.

Questions owners ask

Is RepForce.ai built for trade shops?

RepForce's homepage describes AI sales and support reps for many industries; its 'Industries We Power' list names healthcare, business funding, insurance, real estate, SaaS and others (read October 19, 2026). First Deploy is built only for trade-shop after-hours calls.

What does RepForce cost?

Its pricing page shows a Done For You path without a price, and DIY plans from pay-as-you-go at $0.40 a minute to $2,997 a month, with Enterprise by quote.

What does First Deploy cost?

$1,750 setup ($875 to start, balance at completion), then $250 a month from go-live, with no per-minute charge.

Will First Deploy sell to my callers?

No. By default it doesn't quote prices, promise arrival times or offer discounts. It takes the caller's details, flags emergencies and sends them to you.

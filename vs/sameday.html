When Sameday or Avoca is the better pick

If you run ServiceTitan or similar software and want an AI that books straight onto your board, handles daytime volume and runs outbound campaigns, these products are built for that, and First Deploy isn't.

When First Deploy is the better pick

If you're a 1 to 5 truck shop, you book from your phone or a simple calendar, and the gap is calls after hours, you may not need a full AI front office. First Deploy publishes one price, sets up on your existing number, and sends every after-hours call to you right away.
